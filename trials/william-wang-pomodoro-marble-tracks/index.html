How it works
  1. Choose a mode and press Start. A fresh marble run is generated for every session.
  2. The track forms a valid loop: a marble spawns in the Spawn tile, rides the colored track, and returns to it.
  3. A new marble spawns every minute while the timer runs.
  4. Spring tiles launch marbles faster and Chute tiles drop them at speed.
  5. Restart clears the run and stops the timer. Starting again builds a brand new track.

Shortcuts: Space start/pause · R restart · S skip.
