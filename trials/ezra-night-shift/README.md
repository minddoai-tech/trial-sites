# Night Shift: The Last Table

A small single-page horror restaurant simulator.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step or dependencies are required.

## Play

- Seat normal customers to earn cash.
- Turn away anomalies before they enter.
- Unlock dishes in the menu tree and buy restaurant upgrades.
- Draw a logo and rename the restaurant.
- Survive 90 seconds to make it to dawn.
- Walk the restaurant in first person with `WASD` and turn with the arrow keys. Customers have distinct appearances, and anomalies are marked by an unsettling red tell.
- The 3D view fills the screen. Use the `OPEN SHIFT PANEL` button in the upper-right corner to access customer decisions, the shift log, upgrades, menu discoveries, and branding tools.
- After seating a customer, walk to the kitchen and press `E` to collect their order. Return to their table and press `E` again to serve them.
- Buy a table in the shift panel, walk to an open spot in the dining room, and press `P` to place it. The kitchen is a separate back room connected by the hallway.
- Buy `Expand restaurant` to unlock the sealed east wing, add floor space, and increase the restaurant's capacity.

Keyboard shortcuts: `Enter` / `Space` seats a customer, and `Esc` turns one away.
