How to play

  • Move with Arrow Left and Arrow Right. Press Arrow Up or Arrow Down to jump.
  • Eat fruit for 5 points. The fruit is optional, but collecting all 8 makes a stronger score.
  • Jump on little blue guys to defeat them. Touching them from the side costs a life.
  • Leap over spikes and gaps. Reach the flag to win.

Quick restart

Press R at any time to reset the run. The game also pauses safely when its browser tab is hidden.
