# Highway Dodge

Open `index.html` in any modern browser to play.

- Hold **A / D** or **Left / Right** arrow keys to steer smoothly.
- Earn 1 point per second.
- Traffic speeds up as your score rises and cars begin changing lanes.
- Reach **1,000 points** to win; hitting another car ends the run.
- Click **Drive again** after a crash or win to restart.
