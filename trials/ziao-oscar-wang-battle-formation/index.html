How to Play
  • Recruit knights, archers, and catapults, then arrange them on the 20x15 spawn grid.
  • Press Start Battle. Your troops hold their formation and march forward.
  • Press Attack, then drag a rectangle over troops to select them.
  • Click a point to send the selected troops there. They fight enemies they meet.
  • Knights are tough melee, archers shoot arrows, catapults lob explosive rocks.
  • Each enemy you defeat drops coins that fly up to your coin bar.
  • Win a wave and you get 10 seconds at base to spend your coins before the enemy comes back bigger.
  • You can also press Withdraw in battle to retreat to base and keep your survivors.
  • Your army falls when a wave defeats it; you return here to recruit more and try again.
