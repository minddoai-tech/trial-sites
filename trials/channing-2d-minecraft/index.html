⚜ BLOCK SURVIVAL

A 2D Minecraft survival adventure

Explore a Minecraft world with trees, a village and ore. Survive monster waves and defeat the Ender Dragon to win. You have 5 health hearts.

  • WASD / Arrow keys — move up, down, left, right
  • Left-click (hold) — dig / break blocks (ores give XP!)
  • Right-click — place the selected block
  • Ctrl / right-click swarm — attack monsters with your sword
  • E — eat food to restore hunger
  • 1-9 — choose block/tool
  • P — pause / resume
